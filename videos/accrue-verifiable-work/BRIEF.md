## Intent

A dynamic, narrated recording of using the redesigned Accrue product. The story opens with two understandable needs: a founder needs a reliable API before launch, and a developer needs payment secured. Live job #6 is then created, accepted, funded, submitted, checked by Proof Engine, settled after two separate signed pass votes, and withdrawn. The founder and developer are narrative roles for the payer and worker in the real testnet recording. A visible pointer, accelerated action, camera moves, chapter panel, and Deepgram Aura-2 narration keep the task readable.

## Truth constraints

Proof Engine is Accrue's branded verification workflow, not a proprietary foundation model. It uses deterministic checks and a Jev model through BeatAPI; provider details remain visible in the app's technical record. For job #6, model requirements confidence was 94% and human review need was 15%. That exceeds the locked 10% limit, so the automated reviewer abstained. Two distinct reviewer wallets cast signed pass votes; the video makes no claim that separate people controlled them. The contract paid and the worker withdrew 5 AUSD.
