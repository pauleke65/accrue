# Proof Engine walkthrough narration

## intro

One launch. One API.

## create

Before launch, a founder needs a reliable API. The developer needs payment secured. Accrue locks the response, five AUSD, the deadline, and three reviewers.

## accept-fund

The developer accepts those exact terms. Then the founder locks five AUSD for the work and thirty cents for verification. Neither side has to rely on a promise.

## submit

The developer ships the endpoint and pins a GitHub commit and live URL. Now there is evidence the reviewers can inspect.

## proof-engine

Proof Engine checks the commit and API. Requirements confidence is ninety-four percent. The chance that human review is needed is fifteen percent, above the agreed ten percent limit. So the engine abstains rather than guessing.

## quorum

The evidence gets two separate signed pass votes. That reaches the agreed quorum. The developer no longer has to chase an approval message; the contract makes payment available.

## withdraw

The developer withdraws five AUSD. The founder can see the checks and signed votes that led to payment. The proof stays on record.

## outro

Proof first. Payment follows.
